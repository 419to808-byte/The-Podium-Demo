The Verbatim Rule

By the rules of this portal, the highest-voted post MUST be read aloud — word for word, exactly as its author wrote it — and discussed, by a representative of the United States, in front of the entire country. Another post, drawn at random, must be read and discussed the same way.

AI will be used to detect profanity and errors and to suggest corrections to grammar and clearer formatting. It will never steer you: your topic, your position, or your meaning. And once your post is published, it is final.

Keep in mind that your post may be read in front of the entire nation. Write in your most professional manner. Profanity, slurs, and personal attacks will not post. State your idea or concern plainly and professionally. You are not posting a comment. You are addressing the nation — speaking to and for every American. Treat it with the seriousness it deserves.

Even if the committee cannot resolve an issue, it has at least brought light to the concern. That is the fundamental purpose of this platform: to shine a light on concerns and ideas, and to seek to resolve them or implement helpful changes.

Fine print (official register)

All submissions are subject to automated pre-publication review. Submissions containing profanity, slurs, or personal attacks shall not be published. Submissions containing slang or errors materially affecting comprehensibility shall be held pending correction by the submitting party, with suggested revisions provided for consideration; no submission shall be altered by the System without the submitting party’s express action. Weekly selections shall consist of (i) the submission receiving the highest number of votes during the applicable period and (ii) one (1) submission selected by random drawing from among all eligible published submissions. Selected submissions shall be read aloud in their entirety, verbatim, and discussed in the weekly briefing. A published submission is final and shall not be edited or redacted.

Statements of policy, principles, and fundamentals

The purpose of this platform is to ensure that every citizen has the opportunity to be heard by the nation. Citizens will address the nation and speak on its behalf.

  • The highest-voted post and one randomly drawn post are read aloud in full, word for word, by a representative of the United States and discussed in the weekly briefing. No editor stands between author and nation. Nothing is edited after posting.
  • AI detects problems and suggests wording only before publication. It never steers a post's topic, position, or meaning, and never changes, substitutes, or declines a post on its own.
  • Profanity, slurs, and personal attacks are blocked outright and never publish. Slang, misspellings, grammar, and unclear text are held with suggested rewording; the author accepts the suggestion or retypes the text, and AI never changes text silently. Held drafts are not published, do not enter the draw, and do not use the citizen's one-post slot.
  • Once published, a post is final. It cannot be deleted, withdrawn, archived, or hidden.
  • Voting is upvote-only. Each citizen casts one vote per weekly cycle, switchable until voting closes Sunday at 11:59 PM ET.
  • Each citizen may submit one post per weekly cycle.
  • Posts are limited to 630 words.
  • The random draw is tamper-proof: the eligible pool is frozen in a fixed published order at the cutoff, and the position is the NIST Randomness Beacon value modulo the pool size, plus one. The draw is internal only, with no button, replay, or re-roll.
  • The committee prepares answers only. It never alters, substitutes, delays, or declines a selection. It reviews selections one week in advance and routes each answer to the correct branch and level.
  • The committee does not fund. It asks for funds and allocates funds.
  • Committee answers begin as AI drafts that the committee votes on as written — never edited — and approves as the committee, the answerer of record; no individual member is named. AI never delivers a final answer alone and never touches a citizen post. The drafts are public: the public sees the drafts and the vote, not only approved answers.
  • An exact-duplicate post in the same cycle does not create a new post; the citizen is offered an upvote on the existing post, with no option to post it anyway. The draw pool excludes the winner and any post exactly matching the winner. Posts that are similar but not identical receive only an advisory note.
  • The Live Feed shows the current cycle only. Posts from past cycles that were never selected are deleted; selected posts are kept as the briefing record.
  • Posting and voting run from Monday 12:00 AM to Sunday 11:59 PM ET. The pool freezes at the Sunday cutoff, the draw is Monday at 12:00 PM ET, the committee has the week plus a Monday–Tuesday buffer, and the briefing airs Wednesdays at 1:00 PM ET as the fixed opening segment.
  • This Week shows only the most recently aired briefing. Next Week shows the next briefing's selections. A briefing's replay is published when that briefing airs. Prior Posts & Answers and Status Updates list aired selections only.
  • Participation is limited to verified citizens; in this demo, verification is simulated only, and no real identification is collected or stored.
  • Language: a citizen may post in any language. The original post is the official record and is read aloud verbatim in the author's language. Translations are machine aids for readers and never replace the original.
  • Live activity: when ‘Simulate live activity’ is enabled, the feed shows AI-generated demonstration posts, clearly labeled. They are never voted on, never drawn, and never briefed.
  • Mock accounts: signing up stores a local profile in this browser only, with no real identity verification.
  • Real sign-in/sign-up: shown as a preview; the backend and text verification are not yet built.
  • Security: every post carries a SHA-256 hash, and every action is recorded in a hash-chained audit log.

            Committee

            The committee is 12 ordinary verified citizens, chosen by random draw, serving 12-week terms, with one new citizen joining every week. The committee works for the citizen whose post was selected — it reviews and approves the answers, and tracks each one until it is resolved or formally closed with no action. It does not fund; it asks for and allocates funds.

            Committee rules — selection, pay, structure & schedule

            How members are selected

            Twelve ordinary verified citizens, chosen by random draw from a national pool using the NIST Randomness Beacon — the same method as the post draw, proof against rigging. Selection is a summons, not a draft: if your name comes up, you agree to serve or decline and the next citizen is drawn. To serve you must be a verified citizen, 18 or older, with no post in the current cycle, not a government employee, and not currently incarcerated or on parole or probation — a past felony does not exclude you; eligibility returns 5 years after the sentence is fully complete. Terms are 12 weeks, one new citizen joins every week, and after cycling out you sit out 12 weeks. No more than 2 terms per year. No committee member can submit a post while serving on the committee, for the whole 12-week term.

            Pay

            Members are paid $150 per day, for Friday and Saturday ($300 a week). The solo thinking days are not separately paid. Travel, hotel, and meals for the weekly in-person meeting are covered separately at government rates and booked directly — members never pay out of pocket — plus childcare support for in-person days. The pay is $150 flat, no top-up. The plan isn't for committee members to get rich off this — it's more of a public service.

            Structure

            Twelve members, each holding an equal share of the decision-making power. Government agency liaisons advise only and do not vote. Each week the committee receives the selected posts and the AI-drafted answers and votes on whether to follow the AI suggestions. A split vote means no action on that post this week and the post reverts to shine light — it is still read aloud and tracked, never buried. The narrator and the committee spokesperson are permanent salaried roles who present only: the narrator reads posts word-for-word and the spokesperson presents the committee's approved answers. Neither has any authority over posts or answers. If either quits or is fired, the committee hires a replacement. Hiring and dismissal are by committee vote, against public criteria.

            Weekly schedule

            Members receive the selected posts as soon as they are selected and have 3 days on their own to think about them. They all meet in person on Friday to deliberate and vote, and on Saturday to send the agency requests and set tracking in motion. The agency's response arrives Wednesday morning by video call, and the narrator and the committee spokesperson make the script and record live at 1:00 PM ET. Travel and lodging for every member every week are covered at government rates and booked directly.

The community votes — each citizen casts one vote per weekly cycle, switchable until voting closes Sunday at 11:59 PM ET. 3.
